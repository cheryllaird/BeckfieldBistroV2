# Copyright and Takedown Policy

*Effective 17 October 2026*

Beckfield Bistro respects copyright, and we act quickly on valid complaints. This page explains how to tell us about content you believe infringes your rights.

### 1. Our position

Beckfield Bistro is a private recipe organiser. We do not publish recipes, run a public recipe catalogue, or sell content. Users add recipes to their own private libraries, and can share them only with people they choose. Our [Terms of Service](/terms) require users to have the right to save what they add, and to use their library for personal, non-commercial purposes only.

We store content at our users' direction. When we receive a valid notice, we remove or disable access to the content concerned.

### 2. How to report infringement

Email cheryl.laird@outlook.com. Please include:

1. your name and contact details, and, if you act for someone else, who that is;
2. the work you own, for example the cookbook title and page, or the web page and photo;
3. what in the app you believe infringes it, with enough detail for us to find it (for example a shared link, a screenshot, or the account email address);
4. a statement that you believe in good faith that this use is not authorised by you, your agent or the law;
5. a statement that the information in your notice is accurate, and that you own the rights or are authorised to act for the owner;
6. your physical or electronic signature.

Because libraries are private, we may not be able to find content without these details. We will not look through users' libraries in general.

### 3. What we do

- We acknowledge your notice within 5 working days.
- If the notice is complete and valid, we remove or disable the content, usually within 10 working days.
- We tell the user concerned what was removed and why, and give them a copy of your notice. We may remove your personal contact details first.
- If the notice is incomplete, we tell you what is missing.

### 4. If your content was removed

If you believe your content was removed by mistake, or you have the right to use it, email cheryl.laird@outlook.com with:

1. your name and contact details;
2. what was removed;
3. why you believe it was removed by mistake, or why you are allowed to use it.

We will pass your response to the person who complained. We may restore the content if they do not take further action within 10 working days, or if it is clear the removal was a mistake.

### 5. Repeat infringement

We will close the accounts of users who repeatedly infringe copyright, where that is appropriate.

### 6. False notices

Please make sure your notice is accurate. Knowingly sending a false notice or response may make you liable for damages.

### 7. United States users

If you're in the United States, send notices and counter-notices to the same email address. We handle them under this policy.
