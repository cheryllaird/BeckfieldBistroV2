# Privacy Policy

*Effective 17 October 2026*

This policy explains what personal data Beckfield Bistro collects, why, and what your rights are. Cheryl Laird is the controller of this data under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). Contact us at cheryl.laird@outlook.com.

### 1. What we collect and why

| Data | Where it comes from | Why we use it | Lawful basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Name, email address, profile photo and Google account ID | Google, when you sign in | To create and secure your account | Contract |
| Recipes, notes, meal plans, shopping lists and pantry items | You | To provide the app | Contract |
| Photos you take or upload, including photos of recipe pages | You | To read the recipe and show the photo with it | Contract |
| Web links you import, and the page's title, text and image link | You, and the website you link to | To read the recipe and link back to its source | Contract |
| Your Gemini API key (stored encrypted) | You | To run recipe extraction on your behalf | Contract |
| Email addresses of people you share with or invite | You | To deliver the share or invitation to that person | Legitimate interests (letting you share with people you choose) |
| Technical logs: time of request, extraction method, website hostname, errors | Generated by the app | To keep the app working and secure, and fix faults | Legitimate interests |

We also keep anonymous daily counts of which extraction method worked (for example "read from photo" or "read from the page's recipe data"). These counts contain no personal data. When you accept the Terms, we record which version you accepted and when, as a record of your agreement.

We do not use your data for advertising, sell it, or use it to train AI models.

### 2. Who we share it with

| Recipient | What they do | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) | Sign-in and database storage, as our processor | Europe (eur3 multi-region); Google may also process data in the US |
| Vercel Inc. | Hosts the app and runs its server functions, as our processor | United States and other regions |
| Google (Gemini API) | Reads recipe photos and pages when you extract a recipe. This uses your own API key, under your own agreement with Google | United States and other regions |
| People you share with, and members of your bistro | See the recipes and data you share with them | Wherever they are |

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.

### 3. International transfers

Some of these providers process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, we rely on the UK's adequacy regulations (including the UK-US Data Bridge where the provider is certified) or on the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

### 4. How long we keep it

- Account data and your content: until you delete it or your account.
- Recipe shares and bistro invitations that are never accepted: deleted 90 days after they are sent.
- Server logs: kept by Vercel, our host, for a short period (no more than a few days).
- Backups: we keep a weekly backup of the database for 8 weeks, and a 7-day history used only to recover from mistakes.

You can delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete account. This immediately deletes your account, your own bistro and everything in it, your saved API key, and any shares and invitations you sent or received that are still pending. Members of your bistro lose access to it. Recipes you added to someone else's bistro stay there for its members, and a recipe you sent that someone has already saved belongs to them. Deleted data is removed from backups as they expire, within 8 weeks.

### 5. Your rights

You have the right to:

- see a copy of your data;
- correct it;
- delete it;
- get it in a portable format;
- object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use it.

To use any of these rights, email cheryl.laird@outlook.com. We will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office ([ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk), 0303 123 1113). We'd appreciate the chance to put it right first.

### 6. People you share with

If you share a recipe with someone or invite them to a bistro, we store their email address so the share or invite reaches them when they sign in. The app does not email them. If they don't accept it within 90 days, we delete it. Please only enter the email address of someone who would expect to hear from you.

### 7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Your Gemini API key is encrypted with AES-256 before it is stored. Database access is limited by security rules, so each user can reach only their own data and the bistros they belong to.

### 8. Children

The app is not for children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has created an account, email cheryl.laird@outlook.com and we will delete it.

### 9. Cookies and browser storage

The app uses browser storage (IndexedDB and local storage) to keep you signed in and to make your library work offline. This storage is strictly necessary for the app to work, so it needs no consent. We use no advertising or analytics cookies.

### 10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
